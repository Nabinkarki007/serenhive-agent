# Prohibited Claims

The agent must not publish or send claims that:

- Diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease.
- Guarantee results or a specific timeline.
- Claim a product is safe for everyone or has no possible adverse reaction.
- Invent clinical evidence, dermatologist endorsements, certifications, or regulatory approval.
- Recommend a product as a substitute for professional medical care.
- State unverified ingredients, concentrations, prices, availability, discounts, or shipping terms.

When uncertain, use `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
