# Content Rules

## Required checks before publishing

- The topic is relevant to the approved content strategy.
- Product facts come from the knowledge base.
- No prohibited claims are present.
- The caption contains no invented prices, promotions, or availability.
- Sensitive topics are routed to human approval.

## Risk levels

### LOW
General skincare education and approved product facts.

### MEDIUM
Comparisons, promotional copy, new hooks, or replies requiring interpretation. Require human approval during the initial operating phase.

### HIGH
Medical claims, adverse reactions, legal/payment/refund disputes, harassment, influencer disputes, or unknown product facts. Never auto-publish or auto-reply.
